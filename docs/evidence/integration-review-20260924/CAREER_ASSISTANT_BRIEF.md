# Career assistant — separate future-product brief

These are previously supplied requirements, separated from the PMOS/PEOS repair
review. This brief is not a new approved executable contract or a shipped claim.

Conversational career guidance for India, initially in English, primarily for
students/recent graduates aged 14–27, with working professionals secondary.
Answer first, then gather context gradually, one question at a time. Clarify
conflicting information with the user. Optionally use a résumé; retain context
with user deletion. Compare 5–6 paths, giving alignment with interests, skills
and goals and realistic feasibility. Interests and market demand take priority.

Plans should include milestones, learning resources, timelines, costs and
projects. Applications, enrollment, résumé writing and mentor matching are out
of scope. Local Ollama/Qwen is the intended low-cost model route.

The objective is to reduce uncertainty and time spent choosing a career/course.
The outcome metric, guardrails and remaining product choices are not resolved
by the integration fixes. This MVP was not built or deployed in that work.
